# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Business owners** (mostly small and mid-size, Philippines first) who might hire Uriel for a website, business system, AI automation or SEO project. They want proof he can turn a business problem into a working system that pays off.
- **Employers and recruiters** hiring for AI automation, full-stack or SEO roles, checking fit, track record and how he works.
- **Readers** of the writing on AI, automation, tech stacks, SEO, GEO and AEO, marketing and the founder's journey.
- **Event organizers** who might invite him to speak.

## Product Purpose

urielpapa.com is Uriel Papa's personal site: a builder and founder in the Philippines who shares what he learns and takes on a small number of projects. Success is a visitor who trusts the work enough to read on, book the free 15-minute discovery call, hire him, or invite him to speak.

## Positioning

"I see the problem. I build the solution. I grow the business." Uriel goes end to end, from a business problem to a shipped system to growth, and has the numbers from his own agency, VITE SEO Digital Advertising, to show it: he founded it at 21 and grew it to a team of up to 22 before it closed after financial losses in November 2026.

## Evidence and constraints

- Facts about Uriel come only from the site itself and his resume (Papa_Resume.pdf, November 2026). The About page may use the resume's career timeline, team and delivery numbers, client names (Noyona Cosmetics, Icamen Legal) and full skills list. Never invent experiences, numbers, clients, quotes or results.
- Home page results (`RESULTS` in src/config.ts) must link to a page that states the same figure.
- Do not publish his phone number.
- Every link carries a `title` (the build's HTML check fails without one).

## Voice

First person, plain and direct, short sentences, specific numbers over adjectives. Honest about failure (the agency closed) and about limits; no hype.
