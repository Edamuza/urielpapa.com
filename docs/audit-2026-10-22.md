# Site and codebase audit: urielpapa.com, 22 October 2026

**Scope:** the live site (main) and the repo on feat/mvp, which adds 31 news takes, 9 long-form drafts, videos and resources, pagination and the fixes below.
**Macro:** a technical founder's knowledge base on AI, automation, SEO/GEO/AEO, tech stacks and the founder journey, for founders and small businesses in the Philippines.
**Goal:** be recognised as an entity, get posts cited in AI answers, and win work enquiries (`/contact?topic=work`).
**Method:** /semantic-seo-sro (`page_audit.py` on one URL per template of the live site), curl checks of redirects, headers and caching, a script over the built output and the content files, a read-only codebase review, `pnpm audit`, and Lighthouse on built pages.
**Not checked:** Search Console, GA4 and Bing Webmaster data (no access from the Mac), backlinks, and `seo-master-plan.md` (it is in the project Library, which this Mac cannot read; this audit uses the 17 Oct SRO audit and `docs/content-plan.md` instead).

## Verdict

The site's technical and schema layers are strong and are now hardened: every page is static HTML, light (75-110 KB), crawlable by every search and AI bot, and checked at build time for alt text, link titles, broken internal links and Schema.org data. The one blocking issue was security, not SEO: the deployed Astro 7.0.3 had a critical remote-code-execution advisory on a public image endpoint. That is fixed on feat/mvp. The weakest SEO layer is still trust off-site (no brand footprint yet) and the content mix: after this release, news takes outnumber long-form posts 32 to 8, so the Tuesday/Friday long-form queue matters more than more news.
