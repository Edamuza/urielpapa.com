# SRO audit: urielpapa.com, 28 October 2026

**Scope:** the live site (main at 19bfada), Google Search Console (property `https://urielpapa.com/`), Bing Webmaster Tools, Google's results for "uriel papa".
**Macro:** a builder and founder's knowledge base on AI, automation, SEO/GEO/AEO, tech stacks and the founder journey, for founders and small businesses in the Philippines.
**Goal:** be recognised as an entity, get posts cited in AI answers, and win work enquiries.
**Method:** GSC and Bing read in Uriel's Chrome (read-only, nothing changed in either account); `page_audit.py` on the homepage, /about, /blog, a topic hub, a long-form post, two news takes, a project page and two of today's best-of posts; curl checks of TTFB, cache, robots.txt, the sitemap and llms.txt; a check of the content files.
**Data window:** GSC performance 16-26 Oct 2026 (the first 11 days with data). GSC page indexing last updated 23 Oct, before the 60+ URLs and edits pushed since.
**Not checked:** GA4, GSC Crawl stats, Bing Site Explorer (would not load), Bing backlinks and markup insights, the monthly four-question AI prompt test (due 1 Nov per `docs/content-plan.md`). Core Web Vitals has no field data yet ("not enough usage data" on mobile and desktop).
**Script artifacts ignored:** `page_audit.py` reports "H1 missing" and "headline not in visible text" on posts because it treats `<header>` as chrome; the H1 is there, inside the article header. Its "thin" flags on the homepage, /blog and hubs are listing cards, not passages. "Technological University of the Philippines" in every page's schema is the sitewide Person `alumniOf`, visible on /about.

## Verdict

The site is fast, crawlable and well structured, but search engines have barely started reading it. Two things hold it back. First, the hub and listing pages (the Seeds of the content network) are not indexed: Google has discovered /blog, /news, /topics and 5 of the 7 topic pages but not crawled them, and those are exactly the 30 sitemap URLs with no `lastmod`. Second, the entity is still split: Google's result for "uriel papa" shows the pre-rebrand title "Technical Founder", LinkedIn still says "Chief Executive Officer @ VITE SEO DIGITAL", urielpapa.blog ranks third with agency branding, and no external site links to urielpapa.com. Bing is the bigger gap for AI visibility: it has not recrawled since the robots.txt fix, so it shows 0 impressions and 0 AI citations, and ChatGPT search and Copilot draw on Bing.
