Anthropic, the company behind Claude, has filed to go public. On October 26, 2026, it filed a registration statement on Form S-1 with the US Securities and Exchange Commission for an initial public offering on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT." The filing gives the first audited figures for one of the biggest AI vendors: revenue is growing very fast, and so are its losses.

This take is part of my [Tech and Business](/topics/tech-and-business) writing.

## What happened?

Anthropic announced the filing in [a short newsroom post](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec). It says the number of shares and the price range "have not yet been determined," and names Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley as the firms providing the preliminary prospectus. The full [S-1 is on the SEC's EDGAR site](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm). The registration is not yet effective, so no shares can be sold yet.

## What does the filing show?

These figures come from [Anthropic's S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm):

| Period | Revenue |
| --- | --- |
| Year 2023 | US$41 million |
| Year 2024 | US$386 million |
| Year 2025 | US$4,585 million |
| Six months to June 30, 2025 | US$1,163 million |
| Six months to June 30, 2026 | US$16,286 million |

* **Large losses.** The net loss was US$41,965 million for 2025 and US$80,748 million for the six months to June 30, 2026.
* **Large customers.** As of September 30, 2026, more than 11,900 customers paid US$100,000 or more over the trailing twelve months, more than 1,900 paid US$1 million or more, and about 250 paid US$10 million or more.
* **Control stays concentrated.** Public buyers get Class A shares with one vote each. A Founder LLC holds Class F shares with 50.1% of the total voting power, and Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust holds Class T shares that elect a majority of the board.
* **Use of the money.** Anthropic plans to use the proceeds for general corporate purposes, including compute and infrastructure, hiring and possible acquisitions.

## What does it mean for businesses and site owners?

Many small businesses now rely on Claude, directly or through tools built on it. The S-1 shows a vendor that grew revenue about 14 times in a year, and also one that is spending far more than it earns. Both facts matter if your product depends on it.

The voting structure also matters. Public shareholders will not control the company, so Anthropic's founders and its trust keep the final say on direction, including product and data policies like [its enterprise safeguards](/news/anthropic-enterprise-frontier-safeguards). Once it is listed, quarterly reports will make pricing and strategy changes easier to spot.

## What should you do now?

* **Nothing changes for Claude users today.** The products and prices stay the same while the IPO process runs.
* **If Claude is core to your business,** keep a second model provider tested, as you would for any key supplier.
* **Watch the price range.** The next filing will set the share count and price, which shows how the market values AI vendors.
