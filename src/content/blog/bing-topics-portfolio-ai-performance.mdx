Microsoft added Topics Portfolio, a beta feature, to the AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools on October 13, 2026. It groups the queries where your site is cited in AI answers into topics. It also lists queries where competitors are cited and you are not. That makes it one of the most practical free AI citation reports a small site can use.

This take is part of my [SEO, GEO and AEO](/topics/seo-geo-aeo) writing. Bing's AI Performance report is one of the four checks in my guide on [how to check if ChatGPT cites your site](/blog/how-to-check-if-chatgpt-cites-your-site).

## What happened?

Microsoft announced the feature in [Introducing Topics Portfolio (Beta) in AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/10/introducing-topics-portfolio-beta-in-ai-performance/) on the Bing Webmaster blog on October 13, 2026. It builds on the AI Performance report, which already lists the grounding queries that led AI answers to cite your pages. In June, Bing [added Intents, Topics, Citation Share and Compare](https://blogs.bing.com/search/June-2026/New-AI-Visibility-Insights-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Intents-Topics-Citation-Share-Compare) to the same report, covering citations in Microsoft Copilot, Bing and select partner AI experiences.

## What changed, in plain words?

A grounding query is the search an AI system runs behind the scenes to find sources for its answer. Until now you could see those queries one at a time. Topics Portfolio adds a higher-level view.

* **A topic table.** Each topic is a cluster of related grounding queries where your site appears. For each one, Bing shows three numbers: Topic Size (approximate citations across all sites), Sites Cited (how crowded it is) and Queries.
* **Opportunity queries.** Below the table, Bing lists the strongest queries where other sites are cited and yours is not. Citation volumes are shown as ranges, not exact numbers.
* **A topic detail page.** Clicking a topic shows your own citations in it, the queries where you already appear, and that topic's opportunity queries.
* **Feedback buttons.** Every topic and opportunity query has a thumbs up and thumbs down.

You can pick a one, two or three month window. Bing calls the numbers directional signals for planning, and warns that topic labels are AI-generated and can be imperfect.

## What does it mean for small businesses and founders?

Bing Webmaster Tools is free, and this data comes from Bing's own AI answers rather than from a third-party tool sampling prompts. Copilot and Bing AI answers share Bing's index. A topic where you are already cited is a good sign that your pages are trusted on that subject.

The opportunity list is the useful part. A query where three other sites are cited and you are not is a specific brief: write or rewrite the page that answers it. That is far more actionable than a single visibility score.

There are limits. Bing says the tab only appears if your site has enough data, and smaller sites may see a short list or nothing. Competitors' brand names can also show up as opportunities, which you should skip. And Bing covers Microsoft's AI experiences, not ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews.

## What should you do now?

1. **Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools** if you have not, and submit your sitemap.
2. **Open AI Performance and look for the Topics Portfolio tab.** If it is missing, your site does not have enough citation data yet. Check again monthly.
3. **Start with topics where you are already cited.** Bing suggests this too, because those are the subjects where you are closest to winning more.
4. **Turn two or three opportunity queries into page updates.** Answer each one directly in the first sentences of the page that fits it best, then compare the topic a month later.
5. **Test the same queries in ChatGPT and Google**, since a gap in one AI system often shows up in the others.
