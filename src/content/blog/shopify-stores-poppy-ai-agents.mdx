If you sell on Shopify, AI shopping agents can now read your store without you doing anything. Sierra released a standard called Poppy on November 13, 2026, and every Shopify store now publishes a Poppy file automatically. The practical step for a Filipino online seller is simple: make sure your product details, prices and stock are correct, because agents will use them.

This take is part of my [AI, in Depth](/topics/ai "AI, in Depth articles") writing. It follows my take on [Meta's Muse building small apps](/news/meta-muse-spaces-small-business "Meta's Muse can now build small apps, like an order tracker"), the agent that now uses Poppy.

## What happened?

On November 13, Sierra's co-founders Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor [announced Poppy v1.0](https://sierra.ai/blog/releasing-poppy "Sierra's announcement of Personal Agent Protocol v1.0"), short for Personal Agent Protocol. They describe it as a way for "personal agents to interact with companies through business agents, websites, and APIs."

The headline for sellers is that "every Shopify store now automatically publishes a Poppy file," so "merchants don't need to do any extra implementation."

## What changed?

Here is what [Sierra's post](https://sierra.ai/blog/releasing-poppy "Sierra's announcement of Personal Agent Protocol v1.0") says Poppy does:

- **Meta's Muse uses it now.** Poppy "is now on for everyone who uses Muse, Meta's AI agent," which "automatically looks for Poppy first on every site it visits."
- **Agents can shop.** The shopping part handles "finding products, filling a cart, and checking out," as a guest or signed in with the customer's account.
- **Customers stay in control.** Through OAuth sign-in, "customers control what personal agents can and can't do," and the agent asks before certain actions.
- **It builds on existing standards**, "like MCP, OpenAPI, and UCP."
- **Anyone can adopt it.** "Any company can implement Poppy on its own," with an implementation kit and a validator.

## What does it mean for businesses and founders?

My read is that your product data is now a sales channel of its own. When an agent shops for a customer, it reads your titles, prices, variants and stock, not your banner or your photos. A product page with an old price or a "sold out" item that still shows as available will cause wrong orders.

For a Filipino seller on Shopee, Lazada or TikTok Shop only, nothing changes today. If you run your own Shopify store, you are already in. It is the same shift I covered with [businesses claiming their listing in ChatGPT](/news/chatgpt-businesses-claim-listing "ChatGPT now lets businesses claim their listing and see mentions"): AI tools now act on what your business publishes.

## What should you do now?

1. **Check your Shopify product data.** Fix titles, prices in pesos, variants and stock levels.
2. **Write clear shipping and return policies**, since an agent may read them for the buyer.
3. **Watch your orders** for unusual patterns, and note any that came through an agent.
4. **Not on Shopify?** Read Sierra's implementation kit before deciding whether to add Poppy to your own site.
