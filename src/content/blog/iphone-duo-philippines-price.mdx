Apple's first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, went on sale worldwide on October 23 and reaches the Philippines on October 30, from ₱159,990. At that price, the phone is less a gadget than a cost line. That holds for resellers holding stock, telcos activating it, app makers adapting to it, and anyone buying one for work.

This take is part of my [Technology and Business](/topics/tech-and-business) writing.

## What happened?

The first foldable iPhone made its store debut on Friday, October 23, 2026, Apple said in [iPhone Duo arrives at Apple Store locations worldwide](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/iphone-duo-arrives-at-apple-store-locations-worldwide/). Tim Cook marked the day with a [post on X welcoming iPhone Duo](https://x.com/tim_cook/status/2113799050877452321). For the Philippines, Apple's [iPhone Duo page for the Philippine store](https://www.apple.com/ph/shop/buy-iphone/iphone-duo) takes pre-orders and lists the phone as available starting October 30.

## What changed, in plain words?

The iPhone Duo folds open from a 5.4-inch outer display to a 7.6-inch inner display. These are the Philippine prices on Apple's store as of October 26, 2026, which Apple says include all taxes and duties:

| Storage | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 256 GB | ₱159,990 |
| 512 GB | ₱175,990 |
| 1 TB | ₱207,990 |
| 2 TB | ₱254,990 |

For comparison, the same page lists the iPhone 18 Pro from ₱94,990. The iPhone Duo also activates only with an eSIM and has no physical SIM tray, according to the store page.

## What does it mean for small businesses and founders?

**Resellers** carry the most risk. One 256 GB unit ties up ₱159,990 in stock. Apple's own price already includes taxes and duties, so there is less room to undercut it. In my view, pre-orders and deposits make more sense here than buying inventory on hope.

**Telcos** get a new reason to push eSIM. Apple's page says the phone needs a carrier that supports eSIM and points buyers to their carrier for details. Every sale now includes an activation step, so staff and support scripts need to handle eSIM transfers smoothly.

**App developers** get a new screen to design for. Apple's post on [developers building apps for iPhone Duo](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/10/developers-bring-incredible-new-app-experiences-to-iphone-duo/) says the inner display is 50 percent larger than the iPhone 18 Pro Max. Both displays share the same aspect ratio, and Split View lets users run two apps side by side. Optimized apps get a Made for iPhone Duo badge. If your team ships with React Native, my take on [Expo SDK 58 and iOS 27](/news/expo-sdk-58) covers the platform changes underneath.

**Buying one for work** comes down to the ₱65,000 gap over the entry iPhone 18 Pro. That gap pays off only if you work in documents, video calls or dashboards on your phone. Apple lists Zoom, Slack and Salesforce among the optimized apps, with Microsoft Word and Outlook updates rolling out in the coming weeks. If your phone is mostly for chat and calls, I would buy the cheaper model. I would also wait a few weeks for owner reviews before putting ₱160,000 into a first-generation design.
