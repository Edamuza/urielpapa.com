ChatGPT Pro and Business subscriptions now come with free OpenAI API credits, worth up to US$500 a month. OpenAI added the benefit on October 28, 2026, so a team that already pays for ChatGPT can now build on the API without a separate budget, as long as it spends the credits within the month.

This take is part of my [AI, in Depth](/topics/ai) writing.

## What happened?

On October 28, OpenAI's [API changelog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/changelog) announced that "eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscribers can now claim up to $500 per month in free API credits" as a subscription benefit. You activate it from ChatGPT or the API Platform by linking an API organization you own.

The details are in OpenAI's guide, [API credits for ChatGPT subscribers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/chatgpt-api-credits).

## How much do you get?

The monthly credit depends on your plan, per OpenAI's guide:

| Plan | Monthly API credits |
| --- | --- |
| ChatGPT Pro, US$100 tier | US$100 |
| ChatGPT Pro, US$200 tier | US$200 |
| ChatGPT Pro, US$500 tier | US$500 |
| ChatGPT Business, Standard seat | US$20 per paid seat |
| ChatGPT Business, Premium seat | US$100 per paid seat |

Business credits are pooled and capped at US$500 per workspace per month. A Business workspace with five paid Standard seats, for example, gets US$100 a month to share.

## What are the catches?

The guide lists these limits:

- **Credits don't roll over.** Unused credits expire at the end of the billing cycle, and your first grant is prorated for the rest of the current cycle.
- **No Codex through API keys.** The credits also don't raise your ChatGPT or Codex usage limits.
- **Only Pro and Business qualify.** ChatGPT Enterprise is not eligible, and neither are Plus, Go or Free. Free trials and complimentary subscriptions don't count.
- **The subscription must be at least seven days old** and paid.
- **One owner for both sides.** You must own the ChatGPT subscription or Business workspace and the API organization, using the same email address for both.

The guide does not mention any country restrictions.

## What does it mean for small businesses and founders?

For a small team in the Philippines already on ChatGPT Business, this turns part of the subscription into a free testing budget. That is enough to try an AI step in an automation, a customer-support chatbot or an internal tool before committing real money. It pairs well with OpenAI's cheaper models, which I covered in [my take on GPT-6 Sol and Luna](/news/gpt-6-sol-luna).

The monthly expiry is the main thing to plan around. These credits suit experiments and small workloads, not a production app that has to keep running when the credits run out.

## What should you do now?

1. **Check your plan.** Only an active Pro or Business subscription qualifies.
2. **Pick the owner.** Make sure the person who owns the ChatGPT subscription also owns the API organization, with the same email.
3. **Claim it.** In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Billing for Pro, or your workspace's billing settings for Business, and start the API credits claim.
4. **Set a spending limit** on the API organization, so usage beyond the free credits doesn't surprise you.
