GEO (generative engine optimization) and AEO (answer engine optimization) are the practices of making your content easy for AI systems to understand, quote and cite in their answers. They build on SEO rather than replace it: an assistant can only cite a page it can find, fetch and understand.

This explainer is part of my [SEO, GEO and AEO](/topics/seo-geo-aeo) writing. I have worked in SEO since 2023, and this site itself is set up as a working example of the ideas below.

## What are GEO and AEO, in plain words?

Search used to mean a page of links. Now many people ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews or another assistant, and get a written answer that quotes a few sources. GEO and AEO are about being one of those sources.

- **AEO** is about answers. Put a direct answer to the question near the top of the page, phrase headings as the questions people ask, and keep each section focused on one question.
- **GEO** is about how generative systems read your content. Make it clear who wrote it, what entities it covers, what evidence supports it, and where each fact comes from.

The two overlap so much that most people use them together.

## How do AI search systems find and cite pages?

Most AI answers that cite websites follow a similar path: the system searches an index, fetches a handful of pages, reads the passages that look relevant, and writes an answer that quotes or links some of them. That means three things matter:

1. **Access.** The crawler or fetcher must be allowed by robots.txt and not blocked by a firewall or CDN bot setting.
2. **Passages.** Each section should make sense when read on its own, because an assistant may quote only one passage.
3. **Trust.** Clear authorship, sources for figures and consistent information across the web make a page safer to cite.

## What still matters from SEO?

Nearly everything. Pages still need to be crawlable and indexable, load quickly, have a clear title and meta description, use headings that match what the page covers, and link to related pages on the same site. Google Search Console is still the first place to check whether pages are indexed and which queries bring people in.

## What can you do on your own site today?

- **Answer the question in the first two sentences** of each page.
- **Phrase headings as questions**, one question per section.
- **Name things clearly**: people, products, tools and places, using the same names everywhere.
- **Cite sources** for figures and claims, and date anything that changes.
- **Add structured data** (JSON-LD) for the page type and the author.
- **Check robots.txt and your CDN** so the crawlers you want are not blocked.
- **Keep your profiles consistent** so the author entity is easy to recognize.

<Callout type="warning">

Some CDNs and firewalls have their own AI bot blocking settings that override robots.txt. Check them, not only the robots.txt file.

</Callout>
## How is this site set up for AI search?

These are facts about how urielpapa.com is built:

- **robots.txt allows AI crawlers.** It welcomes search and AI answer crawlers, including OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot, and also allows training crawlers such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended. Only the CMS and a few utility routes are disallowed.
- **JSON-LD structured data** on every page type, describing the site, the author and each post.
- **Markdown copies of posts** at /blog/post-slug.md. Each copy points back to the original page as the canonical version and is marked noindex, so it never competes with the HTML page.
- **An llms.txt file** that summarizes the site, its topics and its latest posts for AI tools. It is a proposal rather than an adopted standard, so it is generated at build time and kept simple.
- **A full-content RSS feed** of posts and notes.
- **Direct answers and question headings** in every post, like this one.

## How do you know if it is working?

There is no single report for AI citations yet. Useful signals include referral traffic from AI assistants in your analytics, Search Console data for queries and pages, and simply asking the assistants questions your pages answer and noting whether you are cited.
