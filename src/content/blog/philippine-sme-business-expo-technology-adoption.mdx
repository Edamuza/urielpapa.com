## What is the Philippine SME Business Expo?

The Philippine SME Business Expo, also called PHILSME, is a business expo, conference and networking event for small and medium enterprises, start-ups and entrepreneurs in the Philippines, run by PHILSME Business Expo and Network. It is held twice a year in Metro Manila, usually in May and November, and recent editions have taken place at the SMX Convention Center Manila in Pasay City, as [GMA News reported](https://gmanetwork.com/news/money/companies/927888/15th-philsme-business-expo/story) for the 15th edition.

Each edition brings together about 180 exhibitors offering business solutions such as fintech, logistics and business loans, alongside keynote talks from industry speakers, and the [official PHILSME site](https://philsme.com) expects more than 12,000 attendees. The 18th edition was scheduled for May 22 to 23, 2026 at the SMX Convention Center Manila, according to the [Embassy of India in Manila's trade fair calendar](https://www.eoimanila.gov.in/forthcoming-business-events-trade-fairss).
