## The problem

When a site needs rebuilding, the old pages, their images and their fonts are the starting point. Sometimes the only copy left is on the Wayback Machine, and saving it page by page by hand is slow.

[Uriel to add: the rebuild that started this tool]

## What we built

- **A multi-threaded command-line tool** that saves web pages, including Wayback Machine snapshots.
- **Assets included:** each page comes down with its images and fonts.
- **A WordPress theme layout** for the saved files.
- **A WordPress block child theme base** packaged with the tool, for bringing the saved pages into WordPress.

## Stack and why

- **Tool:** Python.
- **Target:** WordPress, through a block child theme base.
